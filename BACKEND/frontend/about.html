ABOUT OCEANVISTA

Turning ocean data
into discovery.

OCEANVISTA 3D is an interactive ocean visualization platform designed to transform scientific ocean model data into an intuitive and immersive experience.

01 / THE PROJECT

Explore what lies
beneath the surface.

OCEANVISTA 3D brings real ocean model data into an interactive environment where users can explore ocean conditions by location and depth.

3D

A visual approach to ocean science

Instead of working directly with complex scientific datasets, OCEANVISTA presents ocean information through interactive visualizations that make the data easier to explore and understand.

03 / UNDERSTANDING THE DATA

Not every point
tells a story.

Scientific datasets naturally contain areas where measurements are unavailable. Understanding these gaps is part of correctly interpreting ocean data.

01

Empty spots are normal

Where a point represents land or a location deeper than the seafloor, the dataset may contain no value. As a result, the profile may show nothing at that location.

02

Temperature for now

The current implementation focuses on temperature data. Salinity, currents and additional dates are planned for later versions of the platform.

04 / TECHNOLOGY
